# Insurance premium attention (email)

Status: Later (not this sprint)

## Goal

Surface LIC / insurer **premium due** and **payment confirmation** on the same attention path as SMS dues (Home cards, mark paid, daily Telegram). Dhan remains the ledger.

## Why later

No premium-due or premium-paid **SMS** showed up in the backup. Payment confirmation **emails** do exist. Until a Gmail (or similar) importer exists, there is nothing to classify.

## Depends on

- Gmail connector + importer (roadmap Phase 4). Reuse the folder/ingest pipeline; do not special-case a one-off mailbox scrape.
- Then: treat premium-due mail as a due reminder; payment-confirmation mail as the paid ack (same cycle key as SMS dues).

## Keep out of Home

Dhan month income/expense (and pace stats) stay on the **Telegram** daily digest only. Home stays dues, blocked pushes, services, last import. Spend buckets (grocery, shopping, large merchant vs last month) are also Telegram-only, from Narada SMS `financial_events`, not Firefly categories.

## Out of scope until email works

- Calendar-only yearly reminders
- Manual premium schedule without a message
- Putting Firefly charts on `GET /`
