## Goal

Ingest SMS messages from SMS Backup & Restore exports and convert financial messages into normalized Narada events.

This is the first step toward building financial intelligence capabilities on top of Narada.

---

## Context

Many important financial events already arrive through SMS:

- Credit card transactions
- UPI transactions
- ATM withdrawals
- Utility bill reminders
- Credit card due reminders
- Salary credits
- Bank debits and credits

Narada should be able to process these messages and store them as structured events.

---

## Input Source

SMS Backup & Restore XML export

Example:

```xml
<sms
  address="HDFCBK"
  date="..."
  body="Rs. 4,532 spent on HDFC Bank Credit Card ending 1234 at AMAZON."
/>
